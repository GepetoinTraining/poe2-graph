# Trade — Hideout Is Lava vs Hideout Warrior

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The **time-allocation discipline** between time spent in the hideout (crafting / trading / sorting / managing) vs time spent on maps (farming).

Pedro's framing is the named tension:

- **"Hideout is lava"** — minimize hideout time. Every minute in hideout is a minute not farming. Trade from the map portal; don't stop to organize.
- **"Hideout warrior"** — embrace hideout as the actual engine. Crafting, flipping, project management — your map runs feed it.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- When hideout-is-lava wins:
  - League start through Week 2 (map yields are dense)
  - Build is still gear-progressing (mapping IS the build engine)
  - Juiced strategies that need sustained runtime
  - Fast-clearing builds where map velocity dominates per-hour return
- When hideout-warriorhood wins:
  - Late league (crafting profitability passes mapping yields)
  - During named projects (mirror crafts, specific top-end builds)
  - Fatigue management (crafting is lower-twitch than juicing)
  - When your build is at clear-speed plateau and gear bottlenecks the next step
- The mode-switch trigger: what signals tell you to switch (drop trend, project deadline, energy level)
- Anti-pattern: defaulting to one mode without considering the other
- Anti-pattern: hideout-warriorhood as procrastination (organizing the stash isn't progress)
- The "20% hideout, 80% map" or "50/50 split" or pure-extreme practices among top players

This is one of the **most personalized 1% discussions** — different players default differently. The skill is making the choice **deliberately**, not by inertia.
