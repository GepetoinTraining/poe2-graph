# Farming — Boss Rushing

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The discipline of **skipping clear** and racing to the boss for boss-specific loot. Trade-off: faster per-map time vs missing clear rewards.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The boss-rush decision: which bosses have valuable enough drops to skip clear
- The map-layout discipline: identifying boss location early
- The "rush, but engage on the way" patterns (touching specific mechanics that don't require clearing the whole map)
- Citadel rushing vs full-clear citadel runs
- Pinnacle boss rotation (kill the same boss 10x for the drop pool)
- The IIQ/IIR vs raw-boss-kills trade-off (rushing skips the magic-find layer)
- Boss-rush builds vs general-purpose builds (some builds are tuned specifically for the rush)
- When boss-rushing is wrong: when clear has its own EV+ (essence farming, specific atlas mechanic farming)

Boss rushing **inverts the standard farming loop** — instead of feeding maps to harvest content, you feed maps to harvest a single output.
