# Path of Exile 2 0.4 — The Last of the Druids (December 12, 2025)

The Druid class debut + an Incursion-flavored Vaal league mechanic.

## League mechanic — Fate of the Vaal

Inspired by PoE 1's Incursion. The loop:

1. **5 Vaal Remnants** scatter per zone — circular structures with monsters standing on top.
2. **Kill the monsters** standing on a remnant → unlock passage to the **Vaal Ruins**.
3. **Vaal Ruins** is a grid-map interior: a starting room, **Atziri's Chambers** at the far end, and empty grid slots between.
4. **Place rooms** — each trip allows placement of **6 new rooms** that you then immediately explore. Rooms carry encounter types and reward archetypes.
5. **Reach Atziri's Chambers** through the room layout you built up.

**Rewards**:
- **Double Corruption** chamber — applies two corruption rolls in sequence (huge for endgame crafting).
- **Vaal Prosthetic** — replace one of your limbs (slot-equipped) with a powerful Vaal-themed prosthetic. New itemization vector.

## The Druid class

Shapeshifting hybrid. Four forms enable **hybrid melee-caster builds** by swapping between forms mid-combat.

- **Talisman weapons** — new two-handed martial weapon class used for shapeshifting attacks. Each Talisman shapeshifts the Druid into a specific animal form and grants a basic attack skill for that form.
- **Four shapeshift forms** — each carries its own combat identity and skill set.
- **Hybrid scaling** — Druid scales both attack and spell sources cleanly, which is unusual.

## Content additions

- 11 new unique items
- 9 new lineage supports (new support-gem family)
- 21 reworked support gem lines
- 90+ new active skills
- 250+ new passive nodes (atlas + tree)

## Player-impact notes

- 0.4's **room-placement mechanic** is the most engagement-dense league surface PoE 2 has shipped. Returning players who skipped 0.4 should know it has a real learning curve — not pick-up-and-play.
- **Vaal Prosthetic** is a major itemization addition that survived into 0.5 — worth flagging when a player asks about endgame uniques or "what should I aspire to."
- **Double Corruption** is the canonical late-game craft surface. Mirror-tier crafters (Belton et al.) plan around it.
- The Druid is the most "weird scaling" class to date — playstyles that don't exist for other classes (shapeshift-rotating, talisman-stacking) are possible. Surface for players who want mechanical novelty.
