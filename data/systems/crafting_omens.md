# Crafting — Omens

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Omens** are crafting modifiers that change the outcome distribution of a single orb application. Used correctly, they convert random rolls into directed rolls.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The omen taxonomy by craft action (omens that affect transmutes, regals, exalts, chaos, etc.)
- Targeted-mod omens (force a specific affix type to roll)
- Tier-locking omens (guarantee max-tier outcomes)
- Reroll-with-condition omens (reroll only if X)
- Omens that interact with fracturing
- Omens that interact with corruption
- The omen-pricing layer (omens are tradable; their market value tracks crafting demand)
- Bait omens (look powerful, are overpriced relative to the deterministic alternative)
- When omens stack and when they don't
- The economics of omen-heavy crafts (omen costs can exceed orb costs)

This is a **mid-late game crafting depth layer**. Beginners over-omen; 1% players deploy omens precisely.
