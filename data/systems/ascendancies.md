# Ascendancies (PoE 2)

The **class-specialization layer**. After clearing one of the two Ascension Trials, your character commits to one of multiple **Ascendancies** that lock in a build identity through up to 8 special passive points.

## How ascendancies work

1. **Pick a base class at character creation** — Witch, Sorceress, Warrior, Monk, Mercenary, Ranger, Huntress (0.2+), Druid (0.4+).
2. **Clear an Ascension Trial** (Sekhemas or Chaos at difficulty 1) → unlock the ascendancy choice.
3. **Choose your ascendancy** from the available options for your class.
4. **Allocate ascendancy points** (earned 2 per Trial difficulty cleared, up to 8 total).

The ascendancy choice is **permanent for that character**. You can't re-ascend without rerolling.

## Class → ascendancy map

Each base class has multiple ascendancy paths (Early Access launched with 2 per class; later patches expanded to 3 for several). A non-exhaustive overview:

| Base Class | Ascendancy options |
|---|---|
| Witch | Infernalist, Blood Mage, (others) |
| Sorceress | Stormweaver, Chronomancer, (others) |
| Warrior | Titan, Warbringer, Smith of Kitava (0.2+), (others) |
| Monk | Invoker, Acolyte of Chayula, (others) |
| Mercenary | Witchhunter, Gemling Legionnaire, (others) |
| Ranger | Deadeye, Pathfinder, (others) |
| Huntress (0.2+) | Ritualist, Amazon, (others) |
| Druid (0.4+) | (Druid-specific shapeshift paths) |

Always check current-patch availability — new ascendancies were added through 0.2, 0.4, and likely 0.5.

## Earning the 8 ascendancy points

| Trial difficulty | Points earned |
|---|---|
| Trial of Sekhemas difficulty 1 | 2 |
| Trial of Chaos difficulty 1 | 2 |
| (Either Trial) difficulty 3 | 2 more (points 5-6) |
| (Either Trial) difficulty 4 | 2 more (points 7-8) |

You can do either Trial in any order — the points are interchangeable.

**Typical pacing**:
- Acts 2-3 campaign: clear difficulty 1 of both Trials → 4 points (the "starter ascendancy").
- Endgame entry: difficulty 3 → points 5-6.
- Endgame fully-geared: difficulty 4 → final 7-8.

## How to choose an ascendancy

The structural advice:

1. **Pick the class first based on weapon + skill preferences** (caster vs attacker, melee vs ranged).
2. **Choose the ascendancy that matches your build identity** — not the one with the strongest passive in isolation, but the one that compounds with your build's archetype.

Example: a Sorceress player wanting to scale **mana stacking** picks Stormweaver (or compatible ascendancy) because the kit synergizes — even if a different ascendancy has nominally bigger raw numbers.

3. **Confirm with the active sub-goal** — if the player's goal is "learn the economy," they don't need a max-DPS ascendancy. If the goal is "kill the Arbiter," ascendancy choice matters more.

## Common mistakes

- **Picking ascendancy by raw passive node power** — context wins over magnitude. A "weak" ascendancy in a synergistic build outperforms a "strong" one in a mismatched one.
- **Delaying ascendancy** — many players reach mapping before completing even difficulty 1 of either Trial. Get the 4-point starter ascendancy in the campaign.
- **Re-rolling characters mid-league for "the right ascendancy"** — usually a misuse of league time. Ascendancy switches feel impactful but pale compared to gear progression.
- **Allocating ascendancy points without a plan** — the 8 points are a build commitment. Read the full ascendancy node layout before placing the first point.
