# Trade — Base Item Level (iLvl)

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**iLvl as the primary base-type price signal**. Two items with the same name and base can have wildly different prices based on iLvl alone — because iLvl gates which modifier tiers can roll.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The iLvl breakpoints per base type (iLvl 73, 75, 78, 82, 84 are common gates)
- Which mods open at which iLvl (max-tier life, max-tier elemental damage, max-tier specific affixes)
- The iLvl-pricing curve per base: usually flat below the breakpoint, sudden jump at the breakpoint, mostly flat above
- Identifying iLvl WITHOUT picking up: drop level vs map level vs monster level
- The iLvl=83 / iLvl=84 / iLvl=85 specifics for endgame crafting bases
- Specific bases where iLvl matters less (uniques, gem-related items)
- Specific bases where iLvl matters MORE (crafting-target bases, high-end armours)
- The "iLvl misread" anti-pattern (listing iLvl 81 as iLvl 82 because you skimmed)
- The market signal of iLvl — sellers who price by iLvl correctly are competitive; sellers who don't undersell

This is **the most-common price-discrimination signal** in PoE trade. Mastering iLvl pricing is structurally underweighted in beginner sellers.
