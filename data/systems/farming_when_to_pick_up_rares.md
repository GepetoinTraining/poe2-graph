# Farming — When to Pick Up Rares

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The **inventory-slot economy** in PoE: every rare you pick up costs:
- Pickup time (animation + travel to corpse)
- Inventory slot (limited capacity per map)
- Identification cost (scrolls / wisdom equivalent)
- Stash-tab time post-map
- Decision-time when sorting (do I sell, vendor, or trash this)

vs the rare's expected sell value. Many rares are **EV-negative** when all costs are included.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The per-farm pickup rule (juiced T15? Only specific bases. Low-level run? Different rules.)
- The "show only valuable bases" filter pattern (filter does the first cut; you do the second)
- iLvl-gated pickup (only pick up an iLvl 82+ chest base; ignore iLvl 79)
- Specific-mod pickup (one prefix visible from a distance signals "worth picking")
- The "vendor recipe" outcomes (specific base + specific mods → vendor for currency outcome)
- The death-of-juice pickup penalty (in juiced maps, every pickup is more juice runtime lost)
- The "leave it for the cleanup pass" pattern (some players double-back; some never do)
- Anti-pattern: picking up every rare "just in case"

This is **directly market-coupled** — rare-pickup rules shift as the market shifts. What's sellable Week 1 is vendor trash Week 3.
