# Farming — Juicing

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Juicing** = layering inputs (waystone mods, tablets, omens, scarabs) on a single map to multiply content density and reward.

The trap: juicing has a **non-linear EV curve**. There's a peak past which more juice produces:
- More monster damage you can't reliably tank
- More density that exceeds your clear speed (mob hp pool out-paces dps)
- More variance (one bad pull = a map full of rewards lost on death)
- More currency spent (juice costs scale faster than juice returns)

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The build-juice-ceiling: how to find your build's optimal juice level
- Per-mechanic juice curves (Breach juices differently than Ritual juices differently than Abyssal)
- The "double-juice" pattern (waystone mods + tablet mods compound — sometimes too well)
- Death-tax math: how many in-juice deaths erode EV (each death costs a portal + xp)
- The "I can clear at this level" vs "I should clear at this level" gap
- Juice as a function of league phase (early league: cap juice low; late league: budget for max juice)
- Anti-pattern: juicing past the ceiling for ego or imitation
- The 80/100 juice rule (same shape as marginal capability thinking — never juice to 80%+ of your clear if it costs 80%+ of your map runtime)

The 1% practice is **knowing your inflection point** and stopping just short of it.
