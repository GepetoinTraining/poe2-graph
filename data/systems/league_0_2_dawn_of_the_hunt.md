# Path of Exile 2 0.2 — Dawn of the Hunt (April 4, 2025)

The first major content patch after Early Access launch. Introduced the **Huntress class** and the **Azmerian Wisps** league mechanic.

## League mechanic — Azmerian Wisps

Glowing spirits that roam zones and **flee when approached**. The play loop:

1. You chase a wisp around an area trying to corner it.
2. If a wisp **touches a monster, it possesses it** — empowering the monster with new abilities, modifying its loot.
3. Killing the possessed monster yields the wisp's reward layer.

**12 wisp types**, each tied to different mechanics and reward categories. Wisps stack — multiple wisps can possess the same monster, layering their effects.

The mechanic rewards **map awareness** over combat: you have to track wisp movement, herd them toward monster packs, and avoid losing them to corners or terrain.

## The Huntress class

Azmeri-tribal spear specialist. Hybrid melee/ranged combat is her signature:

- **Dual-use spears** — thrust for melee, throw for ranged. Single weapon class enables both vectors.
- **Buckler** — small shield that deflects incoming attacks and enables counter-attack chains.
- **Beast Taming** — can convert certain rare monsters into permanent companions with modifier-driven powers.
- **Summon Rhoa** — mountable beast; maintains full-speed attacks while in motion.

**5 new ascendancies** were introduced with the Huntress.

## Other 0.2 additions

- **Rogue Exiles** — high-stakes solo encounters with PoE-1-style exile drops.
- **Unique Strongboxes** — pre-rolled strongboxes that guarantee specific loot types.
- **Spear + Buckler item categories** — fully new base type families.
- **100+ new Unique Items** — many spear-specific and Huntress-themed.

## Player-impact notes

- Wisps were a **mid-engagement** mechanic — not free reward, not punishingly hard. Returning players should expect to learn the herding pattern, not optimize it.
- The Huntress is a **build-creativity** class, not a meta-following class. Taming + Rhoa-riding open weird build directions but require investment to make work.
- Beast Taming as a mechanic is unique to the Huntress and didn't generalize to other classes — it's a class identity feature, not a system the broader game adopted.
