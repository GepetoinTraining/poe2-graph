# Crafting — Off-Hand

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Off-hand crafting covers items that pair with one-handed main weapons or substitute for two-handed bases:

- **Quivers** — pair with bows; no sockets; modifier pool is distinct from weapons
- **Shields** — pair with 1H weapons; carry block + defensive mods + occasionally aggressive mods
- **Foci** — caster offhand; spirit + cast speed + damage modifiers
- **Bucklers** (Huntress / 0.2+) — parry-enabling offhand

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The aggressive vs defensive offhand axis — when each wins
- Quiver mod pools that significantly scale damage (added damage, projectile damage, attack speed)
- The Widowhail + rare quiver pattern (Widowhail doubles quiver mods → a high-roll quiver becomes 2x impactful)
- Shield block + spell suppress dimensions
- Focus crafting for caster archetypes (spirit, %damage scaling)
- The "offhand-as-spirit-source" pattern (sceptres in offhand for spirit budget)
- Offhand-specific mods that don't exist on main-hand
- Crafting offhand before vs after main-hand (sequence affects budget allocation)

Off-hand is structurally underweighted in beginner builds. The 1% knowledge is in **treating offhand as a co-equal craft project** with main-hand.
