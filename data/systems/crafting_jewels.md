# Crafting — Jewels

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Jewels are a distinct crafting surface because most jewel slots are constrained to **2 modifiers (magic / blue)** rather than the 6-mod rare ceiling. This changes the EV math entirely.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- **Blue jewel crafting** — the magic-rarity sweet spot. Why 2-mod jewels often beat 4-mod rare jewels for build-specific scaling.
- **Corrupted blue jewels (top-end)** — corrupting a blue jewel can add a third corrupted mod, creating items that rare jewels structurally cannot produce.
- **Timeless jewels** — jewels with seeded mods that rewrite nearby passive tree nodes. The mod-roll search problem.
- **Normal (rare) jewel crafting** — 4-mod jewels for slot-fillers.
- The jewel-base taxonomy (cobalt / crimson / viridian / prismatic) and what mods each is gated on.
- Jewel socket placement on the passive tree as a build-shape decision (some slots are leverage, most aren't).
- Cluster jewels (if applicable in current patch) and their separate crafting economy.
- The trade economics of jewel crafting — jewels are tradable but specific combos are unsearchable, creating arbitrage.

Jewels are often the **highest leverage-per-currency** slot in late-game crafting because each jewel can carry a build-changing modifier on a cheap base.
