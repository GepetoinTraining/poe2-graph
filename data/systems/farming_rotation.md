# Farming — Rotation

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The discipline of **running multiple farming strategies in sequence** rather than running one strategy until depletion.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The rotation taxonomy — what strategies belong together (input-compatible, output-compatible)
- Phase-driven rotation: week 1 strategy → week 3 strategy → late-league strategy
- Input-driven rotation: farm A produces inputs for farm B
- Fatigue-driven rotation: switching to lower-APM farms when tired
- The 30-minute / 60-minute / 90-minute rotation chunks
- Knowing when a farm is depleted (drop rate / market saturation signals)
- The rotation log — what to track per session
- Anti-pattern: random switching driven by FOMO

Rotation is **the structural answer** to one-farm fatigue and to market saturation that erodes a single farm's EV.
