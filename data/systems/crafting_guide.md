# Crafting Guide (PoE 2)

The umbrella for the **1% crafting practice**. This guide is the framing; the sub-guides linked below are stubs that will be expanded with Pedro's donation across play sessions.

## What "1% crafting" means

Most players treat crafting as **mechanical** — apply orb, see outcome, repeat. The 1% practice treats crafting as **portfolio decisions**:

1. **What capability does this craft enable?** Not "this item will be good" — what specific gating does the item unlock?
2. **What's the EV across the miss distribution?** Not "what's the chance of the hit." Crafting selection is a function of miss-value, not just hit-value.
3. **What's my halt criterion *before* I start?** The first sellable outcome wins. Sunk-cost is the beginner's bleed.
4. **What's the alternative if this craft fails?** Every craft project has a fallback path. Without one, you're gambling, not crafting.

Empyrian, Belton, and Ben_ are the canonical voices for this discipline (see creator catalog). Their content compresses years of "1% practice" into watchable form.

## Routes through the crafting space

Different crafting routes have different shape profiles:

| Route | Determinism | Cost floor | Cost ceiling | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Currency crafting (alch + scour + chaos loop) | low | low | medium | Bulk rares, leveling gear |
| Essence crafting | medium-high | medium | medium | One-stat-mandatory crafts |
| Bench / Well of Souls (Bones) | medium-high | medium | medium-high | Add-a-mod finishes |
| Omen-modified crafts | medium-high | medium | high | Targeted modifier control |
| Fracturing crafts | locks one mod | high | high | Mirror-tier multi-stage |
| Gambling (pure rerolls) | low | low | infinite | NEVER, unless explicit EV+ math |
| Corruption crafts | random | medium | very-high | One-shot ceiling raises |

See the sub-guides for each route.

## When NOT to craft

The 1% practice is partly **knowing when buying beats crafting**:

- **Bulk league-day gear** — alch-and-go for slots you'll replace by day 3. Don't waste exalts/divines here.
- **Items with deep market depth** — if 10,000 6-link bows are listed under 5 divines, a "craft a 6-link bow" project is competing with that market. Buy.
- **Mid-tier mid-mod gear** — common rares with average rolls are cheaper to buy than craft.

When to actually craft:
- **Item doesn't exist in the market** at the spec you need (specific niche mod combinations).
- **Slots where capability gates** another upgrade unlock (top-end belt, top-end body armour for spirit, etc.).
- **Mirror-tier projects** where the spec is bespoke.
