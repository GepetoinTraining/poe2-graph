# Crafting — Martial Weapons

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Crafting weapons used by **martial / attack-based** characters. The mod pool and base properties differ from caster weapons (see `crafting_caster_weapons` for those).

Sub-topics to be expanded across the dimensions Pedro named:

**Damage axis**:
- **Physical scaling** — flat phys, %phys, hybrid phys+chaos
- **Elemental scaling** — added fire/cold/lightning, %elemental
- The phys-to-ele conversion crafts and when they win

**Crit vs hit**:
- **Crit weapons** — base crit, crit multiplier, %crit chance
- **Hit-based / non-crit** — alternative scaling (hit damage, ailment damage)
- The breakpoint where crit beats hit for a given build budget

**Range axis**:
- **Ranged weapons** — bows, crossbows, spears (PoE 2's hybrid spear)
- **Melee weapons** — axes, maces, swords, claws, quarterstaves
- Mod-pool differences across the range axis

**Handedness**:
- **1H weapons** — paired with off-hand options
- **2H weapons** — single weapon, larger base damage, different mod ceiling
- The 1H+offhand vs 2H decision tree

**Attack speed**:
- Attack-speed mods on weapon vs gear
- The attack-speed-per-damage trade-off
- The "weapon AS is build AS" rule (weapon AS scales differently than gear AS)

Weapon crafting is **the highest-DPS-impact craft** in the game. Get the weapon right and the rest of the build follows.
