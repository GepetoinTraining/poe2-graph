# Farming — Gambling vs Focused

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The split between **focused farming** (one mechanic deeply, atlas + tablets + inputs all aligned) and **gambling farming** (running unfocused maps hoping for spike drops).

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The focused-farming shape: atlas tree specialization → tablet matching → waystone density → predictable per-map output
- The gambling-farming shape: unfocused maps relying on rare boss drops, lucky uniques, mechanic streaks
- The EV math: focused farming has higher mean, lower variance; gambling has lower mean, very high variance
- When each is correct:
  - Focused: when you have a build that can clear a specific mechanic at speed
  - Gambling: when you're under-geared and need to "win" rather than "earn"
  - Hybrid: most actual league play
- The danger zone: gambling-shaped farming presented as focused (no real EV+, just hopeful)
- Reading other players' farms — many "I made X divines" posts are gambling outcomes, not strategies
- The "gambling streak" anti-pattern: confusing variance for skill

This is **structurally the same shape** as `crafting_gambling` — the EV distinction matters across both.
