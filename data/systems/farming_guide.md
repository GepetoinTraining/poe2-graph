# Farming Guide (PoE 2)

The umbrella for **1% farming practice**. Like the crafting guide, this is framing; the sub-guides below are Pedro-donated stubs to be expanded.

## What "1% farming" means

The new player farms whatever map drops in front of them. The 1% player **engineers** maps:

1. **Selects a content type to farm** (Breach, Ritual, Abyssal, Wisps, raw mapping, boss farming...).
2. **Configures atlas tree + towers + tablets** to amplify that content type to its peak density.
3. **Rolls waystones for the EV-best modifier count** given the build's clear speed + survival.
4. **Counts inputs vs outputs** explicitly — currencies in, currencies out, per hour.
5. **Switches strategies** when league phase shifts the EV landscape (early-league strategies stop being best Week 2-3).

Empyrian and Steelmage are the canonical voices for this discipline.

## The farming-discipline mindset

| Beginner | 1% |
|---|---|
| "I'll farm whatever drops." | "I'm farming this specific output for this specific reason." |
| "More juice = more reward." | "More juice = more variance + clear-speed cost. The optimum isn't always max." |
| "Pick up every rare." | "Filter for the specific bases + item-level + mod thresholds my craft wants." |
| "Run T15 because it's the highest tier." | "Run the tier where my build clears at maximum density per minute." |
| "Sell at vendor / market low." | "Bulk-sell at market median; flip into the missing input." |
