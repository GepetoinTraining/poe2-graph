# Crafting — Caster Weapons (Magic / Spell Vector)

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Caster weapons (and their pairings) have **fundamentally different mod pools** than martial weapons. This guide covers their crafting paths.

Per-weapon-type sub-topics:

**Wands** — single-hand caster weapon:
- Wand-stacking builds (dual-wield via offhand wand for double mod pool access)
- Specific spell-type modifiers ("+1 to fire spell levels," etc.)
- Crit + spell damage scaling
- Cast speed crafting on wands

**Sceptres** — single-hand caster with spirit base:
- Spirit as the primary identity modifier (sceptres carry implicit spirit)
- Sceptre as offhand for spirit-budget extension
- The sceptre vs wand decision for spell-damage builds

**Staves** — two-hand caster weapon:
- Massive single-roll potential (more mods than 1H)
- Staff-specific affixes
- The staff-vs-1H+offhand decision

**Rune Daggers** — hybrid weapon (martial + caster bridging):
- Why rune daggers cross both crafting families
- Hybrid scaling vectors (attack + spell synergies)
- The rune dagger as a unique build space

**Bows + Widowhail + rare quiver** — the exception:
- Widowhail is a unique bow that "doubles all mods on equipped quiver"
- Crafting a rare quiver becomes the actual weapon craft (the bow is a multiplier)
- This pattern doesn't exist for any other base — it's a Widowhail-specific build space

**Shields / off-hands (aggressive vs defensive)**:
- See `crafting_offhand` for the full offhand treatment
- Caster shields specifically (spell block, spell suppression, +levels)

Caster weapon crafting compounds with **spirit**, **gem levels**, and **cast speed** in ways martial weapons don't. The 1% practice respects those compound effects.
