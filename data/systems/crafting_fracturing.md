# Crafting — Fracturing

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The use of **Fracturing Orbs** (or equivalent mechanism) to **permanently lock one modifier** on an item. This converts that mod from a variable into a constant for the rest of the craft.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The fracture decision tree: when to lock a mod vs. keep optionality
- The "fracture-the-hit, reroll-the-rest" pattern
- Fracturing on T1 mods (most common) vs fracturing on rare unique mods
- Multi-stage fracture sequences (lock mod A → roll for mod B → fracture mod B → roll for mod C)
- Fracture-pricing — fractured bases trade at significant premium
- Fracture interaction with other deterministic tools (essence on a fractured base, omen on a fractured base)
- The bait fracture (locking a sub-optimal mod thinking it's the best you'll get)
- The "fracture is forever" mental shift — there's no undoing a fracture

This is **structural to mirror-tier crafting**. Without fracturing, top-tier multi-mod items would be statistically out of reach.
