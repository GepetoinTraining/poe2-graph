# Farming — Low-Level Mapping

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Counter-intuitive farming at LOW map tiers**. Some outputs are gated to lower tiers — running T1-T5 maps deliberately captures rewards endgame mapping doesn't.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- iLvl gating: some base types only drop with desirable iLvl ceilings at LOW tiers
- The "low-level uniques" pool (some uniques can only drop at specific level ranges)
- Low-ilvl crafting bases for specific crafts (e.g. a chest at exactly iLvl 73 for a tier-locked craft)
- Speed-farming low-tier: at low tier your clear speed approaches map-traversal speed
- The "wash" farm — running low-tier when input prices invert the high-tier EV
- New character / alt prep (leveling supports, base inventory) via low-tier
- Specific PoE 2 mechanics that scale strangely at low tier (some leagues add reward density at lower tiers)
- The "low-tier is also lava" anti-pattern (don't farm low-tier just because it's safer)

This is one of the **most overlooked farming surfaces** because endgame culture pushes "play at the highest tier you can handle." Sometimes the best farm is the lowest.
