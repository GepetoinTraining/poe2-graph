# Crafting — Gambling

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The crafting routes that are **structurally gambling-shaped** — outcomes follow a distribution, and the practice is bankroll + halt-criterion management, not deterministic engineering.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- Identifying when a craft is gambling (no deterministic mod control, random reroll)
- Bankroll discipline — what fraction of liquid currency commits per attempt
- The "one more try" anti-pattern and how to recognize it mid-craft
- EV-positive gambling scenarios (mathematically uncommon but real — corruption gambles on specific items, vaal-temple-style ceiling-raisers)
- EV-negative gambling traps that look profitable (chase-craft fallacies)
- When gambling is the correct route (specific bases / build slots where no deterministic route exists)
- The discipline of NOT going back in after the bankroll exhausts

This is the **anti-pattern of the crafting space** — most beginners do this without naming it. Naming it is the first step to bounding it.
