# Crafting — High-Value Bases

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The discipline of **choosing the base before the craft**. The base item determines mod pool, max tier accessibility, implicit modifiers, and resale floor.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- iLvl breakpoints — which tier-of-mod gates at which iLvl
- Base-type implicits (some bases carry desirable implicits; others don't)
- Required-attribute bases (Str-only, Int-only, hybrid) and what that gates
- Corrupted base sourcing (corrupted bases sometimes carry uncraftable implicits)
- The "shop-for-bases" market layer — bases are themselves a tradable commodity
- Bait bases (look right, are wrong — typically wrong implicits or wrong iLvl)
- Canonical high-value bases per slot: build a reference list with Pedro
- The trade-off: cheap base + craft vs. expensive base + cheaper craft

This is the **first 30% of a mirror-tier project**. Get the base wrong and the rest of the craft is wasted.
