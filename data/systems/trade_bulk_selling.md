# Trade — Bulk Selling

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Listing **stacks of currency or items at a discount** in exchange for fast, predictable sales. The bulk vs retail trade-off:

- **Retail**: list one item at market price; wait days; capture full value.
- **Bulk**: list 100 items at 5-10% discount; sell within minutes; capture volume.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The bulk-tab discipline (dedicated stash tabs marked for bulk listings)
- Bulk pricing formulas (market floor × 0.9 typical; tighter for high-velocity currencies)
- Per-currency bulk velocity (which currencies move fast at bulk discounts vs which don't)
- Bulk-currency vs bulk-items (currencies are easier; items require more curation)
- The "convert as you go" pattern (continuously bulk-sell drops into higher-tier currency)
- Bulk + sniping interaction (bulk listings attract snipers; some patterns specifically target snipers)
- The bulk-listing whisper handling (more whispers, faster pace, decision fatigue)
- Anti-pattern: bulk-selling chase items that should be retail-priced

Bulk selling is the **trade equivalent of farming low-level**: it's about volume + velocity, not max per-unit value.
