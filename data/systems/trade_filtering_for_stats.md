# Trade — Filtering for Stats

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The **trade-site filter discipline** — composing precise stat-and-base searches that surface only items meeting your build's needs.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The filter taxonomy: base type, item level, modifier tiers, count groups, pseudo modifiers
- The "stat sum" pseudo mods (total elemental resistance, total life-plus-ES, etc.)
- Min/max threshold setting (don't over-constrain; you'll filter out near-matches)
- The "count" group pattern (any 2 of these 4 mods at this threshold)
- Saved searches as a discipline (recurring buys / snipe targets / build slot upgrades)
- The interaction with selling — filtering for stats also helps you list YOUR rares competitively (search how others list, mirror the pattern)
- Filter-construction cost (a complex search takes 5 minutes; pays off only if you'll re-use it)
- The "false negative" failure mode (over-precise filter hides items that would have qualified)
- The "false positive" failure mode (under-precise filter buries good listings in noise)

This is **directly market-coupled** with `trade_identifying_drops` and `farming_filters` — all three are filter discipline at different layers.
