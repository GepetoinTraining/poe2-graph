# Crafting — Corruption

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Corruption** = applying a Vaal Orb (or equivalent) to an item, randomizing one outcome from a corruption pool. Possible outcomes per craft:

- **Minor mod added** — a small bonus, item largely unchanged
- **Implicit modified** — base implicit is altered (sometimes massively improved)
- **No change** — item gains "corrupted" tag, locks further crafting, no other effect
- **Brick** — item degraded (reduced quality, mod removed, or rendered useless)
- **Major boon** — rare extreme outcome, can multiply item value

Each item type has its own corruption distribution. The 1% practice is knowing **which items have a positive-EV corruption profile** and which don't.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The corruption-EV table per item type (jewels, weapons, gear)
- **Corrupted blue jewels** — the structural top-end of the jewel space (see `crafting_jewels`)
- **Double Corruption** — Fate of the Vaal mechanic (0.4+) — apply two corruption rolls in sequence
- Vaal Temple-style corruption ceilings (where the upside is built into the system)
- The pre-corruption craft (you finish the craft first, THEN vaal — the order matters)
- Corruption + omens interaction (some omens direct the corruption outcome)
- The bricking-distribution discipline (don't corrupt items you can't afford to lose)
- The "corrupt a backup" practice (corrupt your second-best, keep your best uncorrupted)
- When to corrupt for the lock vs corrupt for the outcome

Corruption is **structurally gambling** but with named upside and a knowable distribution. That's distinct from the open-ended gambling of currency-spam crafts.
