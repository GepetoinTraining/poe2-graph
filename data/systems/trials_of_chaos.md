# Trial of Chaos (PoE 2)

One of two **Ascension Trials** (the other is `trials_of_sekhemas`). Ultimatum-derived. Completing earns Ascendancy Skill Points + Soul Cores + unique items.

## Structure

A series of **up to 10 challenge rooms** with **1-3 boss encounters** along the way.

Each room you enter:

1. **Combat encounter** — clear the room's enemies.
2. **Choose a Tribulation** — one of multiple offered modifiers that make **all future rooms** harder. You cannot decline; you must choose.

The Tribulations *compound* — by room 10, you may be fighting through 7-9 stacked modifiers, each making the run harder than the last.

## Rewards by tier

- **Round 4, 7, and 10 bosses** — guaranteed **Soul Core** drop (see `soul_cores`).
- **Round 10 boss** — drops one of the **Fates** required to challenge the Trialmaster.
- Throughout the run: **corrupted rare items**, **Soul Cores** beyond the guarantees, and trial-exclusive **unique items** (Zerphi's Genesis, Mahuxotl's Machination, Hateforge, The Adorned, Glimpse of Chaos).

## Ascension points earned

Same structure as Sekhemas:

- **4 difficulty levels** of the Chaos Trial.
- Each cleared difficulty grants **2 Ascendancy Points**.

Combined with Sekhemas: up to **8 total ascendancy points** across both Trials.

## The Trialmaster

After collecting Fates from difficulty-4 round-10 bosses, you can challenge **The Trialmaster** himself — a pinnacle encounter exclusive to Trial of Chaos. Drops Trialmaster-locked uniques + scaling rewards.

## Sekhemas vs Chaos — which to do first?

Different difficulty profiles:

| Aspect | Sekhemas | Chaos |
|---|---|---|
| Damage mitigation | Armour + evasion + Honour res | Standard ehp |
| Build dependence | Sensitive — pure ES suffers | Most builds work |
| Reward type | Ascendancy points only | Ascendancy points + Soul Cores + uniques |
| Time per run | Longer (rooms + boons + boss) | Shorter rooms; can be aggressive |

**Common routing**: Most players do **Chaos first** for the build-flexibility-friendly entry, then Sekhemas once gear is settled. ES-stackers and Honour-vulnerable builds may have to wait on Sekhemas until later.

## Common mistakes

- **Picking Tribulations greedily** — the worst-looking Tribulation now may compound badly with later rooms. Read the modifier carefully against your build.
- **Not pacing for 10 rooms** — flask usage, gem cooldowns. Run-management matters more than individual encounter damage.
- **Missing the Soul Core layer** — Trial of Chaos is the primary Soul Core source. If you're not engaging it, your endgame socketables stay at Rune-tier only.
