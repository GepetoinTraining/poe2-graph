# Farming — Counting

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The discipline of **measuring inputs and outputs** per farm. The 1% player doesn't farm by feel — they farm by ledger.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The minimum counting setup: pre-farm stash snapshot, post-farm stash snapshot, time elapsed
- What to count: raw currency drops, items sold, items kept, inputs consumed
- The 10-map sample: smallest meaningful sample for an EV estimate
- Per-hour metrics vs per-map metrics
- Variance discipline — high-variance farms need bigger samples
- The "trend down" signal: when the average drops over a rolling window, it's time to rotate
- External tools that count for you (PoB ladder, third-party trackers) — what they catch and what they miss
- The danger of conviction-based counting (only counting good runs)

Counting is the **foundation underneath rotation** — without counting, rotation is just vibes.
