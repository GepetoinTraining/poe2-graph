# Trade Guide (1% Practice in PoE 2)

The umbrella for **advanced trade practice**. The basic surface — async trade, chat trade, listing — lives in [trade_engagement](trade_engagement.md). This guide is the **1% practice** layer on top: bulk operations, flipping, sniping, projects.

## What "1% trade" means

The default-player relationship to trade is **transactional** — buy when you need; sell when something nice drops. The 1% practice is **operational** — trade as a continuous engine running alongside farming:

1. **Bulk operations** — moving large quantities of currency at predictable spreads.
2. **Flipping** — buying undervalued, listing at fair, capturing the spread.
3. **Sniping** — catching items mispriced below market within minutes of listing.
4. **Currency-pair trading** — divine ↔ exalt ↔ chaos at favorable ratios.
5. **Project orientation** — every divine stacked is for a named goal, not a generic "save."
6. **Identification discipline** — knowing when to identify rares (price discovery) vs sell unidentified.
7. **Filter discipline** — your loot filter is also your trade-prep filter.
8. **Time allocation** — the hideout-vs-map decision is a 1% variable.

Belton, Empyrian, and Ben_ each demonstrate slices of this practice — see creator catalog.

## The hideout-warrior paradox

Pedro's framing: **"hideout is lava vs. hideout warrior."**

- **"Hideout is lava"** — minimize time in hideout. Every minute in hideout is a minute not farming. Make trades from the map portal; don't stop to organize the stash.
- **"Hideout warrior"** — invert. Some leagues, hideout *is* the farm. Crafting, flipping, project management. Your map runs feed the operations.

Both are correct. **The 1% player chooses deliberately** which mode they're in for the session.

When hideout-warriorhood wins:
- Mid-late league, when crafting profitability scales above mapping yields.
- During specific projects (mirror crafts, top-end rolls) where micro-decisions matter.
- When fatigued — crafting is lower-twitch than fast clearing.

When hideout-is-lava wins:
- League start through Week 2 — mapping yields are dense, hideout time is dead time.
- During juiced strategies that demand sustained map runtime.
- When the build is mid-progression — mapping itself is the build-completion engine.
