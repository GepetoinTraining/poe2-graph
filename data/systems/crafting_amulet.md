# Crafting — Amulets

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Amulets are a **dual-archetype slot**. Pedro named the two canonical archetypes explicitly:

**Spirit amulet** — amulets where the load-bearing modifier is +spirit:
- Spirit-bumping amulets unlock additional auras / heralds / meta-gems
- The spirit amulet competes with body armour spirit for slot priority
- Specific high-tier spirit amulets (uniques, rare-with-top-tier-spirit)
- The "spirit ceiling" — when adding more spirit no longer adds capability

**Level amulet** — amulets where the load-bearing modifier is +levels to skills:
- "+X to all spell skill gems" (caster scaling)
- "+X to fire / cold / lightning / chaos gems" (damage-type scaling)
- "+X to a specific gem" (build-around modifier)
- Level amulets compound with quality + cuts on the actual gems

Other modifier vectors:
- Life + resists (filler-tier, when neither spirit nor levels is the priority)
- Specific implicits (Talisman amulets, regional variants)
- Multi-stat amulets for hybrid builds

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The spirit-vs-level decision tree per build archetype
- When neither is the priority (rare — usually a build with bigger ceiling elsewhere)
- Anointment / oil interaction (if applicable in current patch)
- Talisman amulets and their unique mod pool
- Implicit modifications on amulets (corrupted, specific base types)

The amulet is one of the **highest-variance** slots — the right amulet can change build identity entirely.
