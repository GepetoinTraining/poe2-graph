# Path of Exile 2 0.1 — Early Access launch (December 2024)

This is the **baseline** every later patch builds on. When a returning player says "I last played at launch," they mean this version.

## What shipped in 0.1

**Campaign (Acts 1–3)** — Three full acts plus their Cruel difficulty reprise. The campaign was the gating for ascension and endgame entry.

**Classes (6 at launch)** — Witch, Sorceress, Warrior, Monk, Mercenary, Ranger. Each with 2 (later 3) ascendancies available in Early Access.

**Ascension via two Trials**:
- **Trial of the Sekhemas** (Sanctum-derived roguelike with the Honour bar)
- **Trial of Chaos** (Ultimatum-derived, Tribulation-stacking)

You complete a difficulty of either to earn 2 ascendancy points. Four difficulties per Trial → up to 8 ascendancy points total. (See `trials_of_sekhemas` and `trials_of_chaos` system guides.)

**Endgame Atlas** — Map-tier mapping with the Precursor Tower / Tablet mechanic gating content density (see `atlas_towers_and_tablets`). Atlas passive tree existed but was smaller than what 0.5 ships.

**Pinnacle bosses** — Citadels (Iron, Copper, Stone) → Crisis Fragments → The Arbiter. The King in the Mists (Ritual pinnacle) and other pinnacle encounters tied to specific atlas mechanics also shipped.

## Cosmetic / quality-of-life things missing at 0.1

- **Sprinting** — not in the toolkit; added in 0.3.
- **Asynchronous trade** — chat-based trade only; the modern async system arrived in 0.3.
- **Instant weapon swap** — weapon swap had a delay; made instant in 0.3.

## Why this matters for the player

- "I played at launch" usually means the player has the **structure** but not the **systems polish** of current patches. Walk them through 0.3's trade/sprint/swap upgrades + 0.5's Fortress + expanded Atlas before assuming they know.
- Builds from 0.1 are usually obsolete — balance has moved repeatedly. Don't surface 0.1-era builds as authoritative for 0.5+.
- The Trials of Sekhemas and Trial of Chaos mechanics shipped at 0.1 and have been refined but not replaced — they are the canonical ascension path through every patch.
