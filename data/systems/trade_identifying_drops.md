# Trade — Identifying vs Selling Unidentified

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

The **identify-first vs sell-unidentified** decision per rare item. Each has costs:

- **Identify** = wisdom scroll + price-search time + listing time, in exchange for known sell price
- **Unidentified** = bulk-listed at chance-tier price (e.g. 1c per rare), in exchange for zero per-item time but very low per-item value

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The bulk-rare market (some buyers want unidentified rares specifically for chance-orb gambling)
- Identify-thresholds by base (high-value bases ALWAYS get identified; cheap bases bulk-sold)
- The "identification reveals option value" frame — sometimes the mod combination is worth more than the base would suggest
- Identification + filtering compounding (filter shows rare → identify → trade-filter shows it's listed-worthy)
- The "stash-tab full" forcing function (sometimes you bulk-sell unidentified because the alternative is dropping the item)
- League-phase identification (early-league time-poor; late-league time-rich)
- Anti-pattern: identifying every rare reflexively (time bleed)
- Anti-pattern: bulk-selling specific bases that are always worth identifying (giving away EV)

The 1% practice is **per-base rules**, not blanket policy.
