# Trade — Flipping

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Buy-low, list-fair**. Capturing the spread between underpriced listings and fair market value.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The flipping shape: identifying a fair-market price, buying anything listed substantially below it, re-listing at fair
- The flip-tab dedicated stash discipline (separate inventory; don't mingle with personal gear)
- Categories with reliable spreads: bulk currency, specific consumables, popular leveling uniques
- The flip-vs-buy decision (sometimes the underpriced item is genuinely worth more to you to USE than to flip)
- Velocity discipline: flipping items that don't move sit in stash forever
- Spread thresholds (a 10% spread isn't worth flipping; 20%+ is)
- League-phase flipping (early-league flipping is easier — more listing errors, less price discovery)
- The diminishing returns curve (the more flippers, the smaller spreads available)
- Anti-pattern: chasing flips on items you don't understand the floor of

Flipping requires **market understanding** — pricing instinct, not just orb counting.
