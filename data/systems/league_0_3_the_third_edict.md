# Path of Exile 2 0.3 — The Third Edict (August 29, 2025)

The most structurally significant patch of the Early Access period. Added a full new act, the first asynchronous trade system, and the **Rise of the Abyssal** league mechanic.

## League mechanic — Rise of the Abyssal

Echoes PoE 1's Abyss mechanic. Maps generate **Abyssal fissures** — cracks in the ground that spawn undead hordes when triggered. Loop:

1. **Trigger fissures** in a map by walking near them or completing prerequisites.
2. **Kill the undead waves** that emerge — they get progressively harder.
3. **Loot Abyssal Bones** — a new currency type dropped by Abyssal monsters.
4. **Craft at the Well of Souls** — apply Bones to existing items to inscribe an extra modifier chosen from a pool of 3.

The crafting-currency framing is similar to PoE 1's crafting bench but with the **roll-from-pool** mechanic that gives players a calculated upgrade rather than a guaranteed-but-bad addition.

**Depth bosses** and a pinnacle Abyssal encounter were added as the mechanic's progression ceiling.

## Structural additions

- **Act IV** — a full new campaign act extending the storyline beyond Acts 1–3.
- **Three narrative Interludes** — short bridges between campaign and endgame, expanding lore for returning players.
- **Asynchronous trade** — the modern trade UI: list items, get matched offers, complete trades without being live in your hideout. Replaced (most of) the chat-based trade flow that dominated 0.1–0.2.
- **Sprinting** — added to the core movement toolkit. Every class gained the ability to sprint by default.
- **Instant weapon swap** — zero-delay swap between weapon sets (see `weapon_sets`).
- **Support gem overhaul** — substantial rebalancing across the support-gem catalog; many gems renamed or redesigned.

## Player-impact notes

- 0.3 is the patch that **made PoE 2 modern**. Async trade, sprinting, and instant weapon swap are the foundational quality-of-life features later patches build on.
- Bones crafting at the Well of Souls **survives** into 0.5 as a permanent crafting surface (not just a temporary league mechanic) — the league mechanic graduated to a permanent system. Worth knowing for any crafting-curious player.
- Act IV is canonically permanent — campaign progression in 0.5+ assumes you walk through Acts 1-4.
