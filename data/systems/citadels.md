# Citadels (PoE 2)

Atlas regions that contain **Uber Act Bosses** and gate access to the final pinnacle encounter — **The Arbiter**. Endgame progression milestone.

## What a Citadel is

A **Citadel** is a special atlas node — visually distinct from regular map nodes. There are three Citadels:

- **Iron Citadel**
- **Copper Citadel**
- **Stone Citadel**

Each Citadel houses **a single Uber Act Boss** with their Lieutenants. Defeating the Uber Act Boss yields one of three **Crisis Fragments** required to unlock the Fortress and challenge the Arbiter.

## Uber Act Bosses

These are super-powered versions of campaign Act bosses. Same general moveset as the campaign fight, but:

- Significantly higher damage output
- Additional phases / mechanics layered on top
- Tighter dodge windows
- Lieutenants alongside the Uber Boss for added pressure

Practically: campaign-level builds don't survive Uber Act Bosses. You need endgame gear + ascendancy completion + atlas optimization.

## Citadel-to-Arbiter pipeline

1. **Find a Citadel** on the atlas (they spawn at specific atlas distances from the map start).
2. **Clear the Citadel** → reach the Uber Act Boss.
3. **Defeat the Uber Act Boss** → receive a **Crisis Fragment**.
4. **Repeat for the other two Citadels** → collect three different Crisis Fragments (Iron, Copper, Stone).
5. **Use the three Crisis Fragments** at the appropriate atlas hub to unlock **The Fortress** (0.5+) / **The Arbiter encounter**.
6. **Fight The Arbiter** — the final pinnacle of PoE 2.

This is the canonical endgame gating sequence — multiple Citadel clears are required before Arbiter access.

## The Arbiter

The final Pinnacle Boss for PoE 2. Lives in **a sealed Fortress, protected by a gigantic maze**, locked behind the **three Crisis Fragments**.

- Highest difficulty pinnacle encounter
- Drops top-tier uniques exclusive to the Arbiter fight
- Multi-phase fight with environmental mechanics

In 0.5, **The Fortress is the new endgame hub** — completing a Precursor Tower for the first time unlocks Fortress access. Citadels still gate the Arbiter encounter specifically (via Crisis Fragments), but the Fortress hub is now the broader endgame center (see `league_0_5_return_of_the_ancients`).

## Other pinnacle bosses (not gated by Citadels)

The Arbiter is the final pinnacle, but PoE 2 has **other pinnacle encounters** tied to specific atlas mechanics:

- **The King in the Mists** — pinnacle for the Ritual mechanic. Access via "An Audience With the King" invitation (Favour reward in Ritual encounters).
- **Mechanic-specific pinnacle encounters** for Breach, Abyssal (0.3+), Wisps (0.2+), etc.

Each carries its own atlas-mechanic-specific drop pool.

## Common mistakes

- **Engaging Citadels under-geared** — the Uber Act Bosses are designed for endgame-completed builds. Trying one with mid-mapping gear is a death-spiral.
- **Skipping ascendancy completion** before Citadels — your full 8 ascendancy points materially change the fight.
- **Treating Citadels like regular maps** — they're not single-room boss fights; they're full atlas regions with their own progression.
- **Confusing The King in the Mists with The King of the Mists** — different. King in the Mists is the Ritual pinnacle. King of the Mists is a story-context boss in the Wildwood.
