# Trade — Sniping

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

**Catching mispriced listings before the seller notices their mistake** — or before other snipers do. The high-velocity, high-attention version of flipping.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- Saved searches — what to set up, what filters to use, what notifications to enable
- The "live the search" attention pattern vs the "check periodically" pattern
- Categories that get mispriced reliably (new players, new items, league-day-1 chaos)
- The sniper's checklist (verify before buying — sometimes the "snipe" is a typo trap)
- Bid-and-clean: snipe → relist for fair → recapture spread
- Time-sensitivity: an undermarketed listing's lifetime is short
- Anti-pattern: snipe-buying items you don't understand the floor of
- Anti-pattern: sniping at the cost of farming (sniping is high-attention; it pays only above a threshold)
- The ethics question (some communities frown on aggressive sniping; some see it as market efficiency)
- The "snipe-as-service" pattern (snipe for someone else's build need)

Sniping is **the most rewarding-per-attention trade activity** when targeted; the most distracting-per-attention when untargeted.
