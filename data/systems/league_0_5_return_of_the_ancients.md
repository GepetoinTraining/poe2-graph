# Path of Exile 2 0.5 — Return of the Ancients (May 29, 2026)

The **largest and most transformative** Early Access expansion yet. Launches May 29, 2026 at 1pm PDT (8pm UTC) on PC + consoles. Comes with the **Runes of Aldur** Challenge League.

## League mechanic — Runes of Aldur (Ezomyte Runesmithing)

A crafting league. The loop:

1. **Ezomyte Remnants** spawn in every zone — objects with **2 to 10 slots**.
2. **Add Runeshapes to a Remnant** — each Runeshape inscribed modifies the resulting item AND spawns more monster waves with **runic buffs** that empower the waves you face.
3. **Runic Ward** is the new resource: Runes consume Runic Ward instead of Mana, with no Attribute or Weapon requirements. **Any class can use any rune** — fully democratized.
4. **Complete the wave sequence** to finalize the inscribed item.

The trade-off shape is clear: **more inscribed runeshapes = better item + harder monster waves**. Players choose their depth.

## The Fortress + endgame overhaul

**The Fortress** — a hub that rises from the earth after completing your first **Precursor Tower** map (see `atlas_towers_and_tablets`). All endgame progression now flows through here.

**Atlas Passive Skill Tree expanded** — over **300 nodes** (significantly more than 0.4). Diverse biome variants and farming archetypes.

**5 brand-new Questlines** — existing mechanics have been wrapped in their own questline + area + crafting choices + endgame bosses. All league mechanics on the Atlas now have **quests that introduce the mechanic and guide you through its stages up to the Pinnacle Boss**. This is the major engagement-design change: explicit progression hooks for every farm.

## Content additions

- **30+ new maps** with diverse biomes — arid Maraketh deserts, lava-filled dungeons, etc.
- **40+ new Unique Items**
- **12+ revised existing uniques** (re-tuned for the new endgame)
- **Atlas tree at 300+ nodes** (up from ~200 in 0.4 era)

## Player-impact notes

- **Returning players coming back for 0.5** will find a mostly-new endgame. The Fortress + expanded atlas + questline-wrapped mechanics are a coherent overhaul, not just additive content.
- **The Runes of Aldur league mechanic is class-agnostic** — Runic Ward / no attribute requirements means a player can engage with the crafting layer regardless of their build. Lower friction than past league mechanics that gated on class fit.
- **Questline gating** means the optimal early-league flow is more *prescribed* than past leagues — knowing which questline to chase first matters more.
- **Runes consume Runic Ward** — this is a brand-new resource. Don't conflate with Mana, Spirit, or other existing pools.
