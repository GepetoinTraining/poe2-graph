# Trade — Currency Trading

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Trading **between currency types** — divine ↔ exalt ↔ chaos ↔ regal ↔ vaal etc. — at favorable ratios. The pair-trade analog of forex.

Sub-topics to be expanded:
- The dominant currency pairs and their typical ratios per league phase
- Bulk currency listing for pair trades
- Reading the ratio chart (poe.ninja or equivalent) as a trend, not a snapshot
- The "currency cycle" — early-league chaos is expensive, late-league chaos is cheap
- Holding the "right" currency for what you need next (anticipating purchases ahead of time)
- The ratio spread (bid-ask gap) — sometimes large enough to flip profitably
- Multi-leg trades (chaos → exalt → divine vs direct chaos → divine, the cheaper path varies)
- Anti-pattern: hoarding the "trending up" currency without an exit plan
- Anti-pattern: ratio gambling (buying because the ratio "should" go a direction)
- The interaction with farming output (your farm produces specific currencies; trade to what you need)

This is the **most market-like surface** in PoE trade. It rewards pattern recognition + discipline.
