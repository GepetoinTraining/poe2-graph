# Crafting — Rings + Belts

**Status: stub.** 1%-knowledge topic Pedro is donating.

## Scope

Rings and belts have a **two-mode** crafting profile:

- **Filler mode** — cheap res + life + small damage = baseline endgame readiness
- **Top-end mode** — specific implicits, +max-res modifiers, rare base types, mirror-tier projects

Sub-topics to be expanded:

**Rings — filler tier**:
- Magic ring with 2 resists + life = league-day-1 ready
- Rare ring with 3-4 mods = mid-mapping ready
- Standard mod priorities (life, %res, +stats)

**Rings — top-end**:
- Iron / Two-Stone / Prismatic / Diamond base implicits and which build wants which
- +max-resistance rings (rare, high-value crafts)
- +X to skill levels rings (caster builds)
- Specific unique rings that change build identity (Stormshroud, etc.)
- Multi-implicit rings (corrupted, specific bases)

**Belts — filler tier**:
- Heavy / Mageblood-style belts at the cheap end
- Standard mod priorities (life, %res, +stats, flask effect)

**Belts — top-end**:
- Specific belt bases that gate top-end mods (Mageblood + tier-1 equivalents)
- Strength-stacking belts
- Charm-slot belts (PoE 2 charm interaction)
- Mirror-tier belt projects

The dimension Pedro named: **"res+life+fillers / topendcrafting"** — there's a clear floor (the filler) and a clear ceiling (top-end), and the 1% practice is knowing **when each slot is worth pushing to the ceiling**.
